# Clichê: 32-bar AABA bossa nova

**[Open in Toy MIDI](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fcliche%2Fproject.json)**

D minor / F major, 130 BPM, 4/4, 128 beats. The whole piece loops, so bar 32 leads back into bar 1. Tracks are flute melody, tenor sax countermelody, nylon guitar comping, acoustic bass, and a GS Brush kit split into two tracks.

## Bar-by-bar plan

| Section | Bars | Harmony | Arrangement |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 1–4 | `Dm(add9) \| Dm(maj7) \| Dm7 \| Dm6` | Line cliché D–C♯–C–B in the guitar's inner voice over a D bass pedal. Flute states the theme. Drums play brush swirl, hat foot on 2 and 4, and side-stick clave. |
| A1 | 5–8 | `Gm9 \| C13 \| Fmaj9 \| Em7♭5 E♭7♯11` | Compound-line melody over a ii–V–I in F. E♭7 is a tritone sub for A7 and leads back to Dm. Brush fill on the last beat. |
| A2 | 9–14 | Same as bars 1–6 | Hats move to 8ths. Tenor sax countermelody enters by doubling the cliché line, then follows guide tones. |
| A2 | 15–16 | `Fmaj9 \| Cm9 F13` | ii–V into B♭ to set up the bridge. Fill into bar 17. |
| B | 17–20 | `B♭maj9 \| E♭m9 A♭13 \| B♭maj9 \| Am7♭5 A♭7♯11` | Backdoor ii–V (iv–♭VII7) into B♭, then a tritone sub A♭7 for D7 into Gm. Drums move to ride plus brush snare comping. Sax sustains guide tones. |
| B | 21–24 | `Gm9 \| B♭m9 E♭13 \| Fmaj9 \| Em7♭5 A7♭9♭13` | Backdoor ii–V into F, then the regular turnaround to Dm. |
| A3 | 25–30 | Same as bars 1–6 | Sax returns on the cliché line. Light snare comping. |
| A3 | 31–32 | `Fmaj9 \| Em7♭5 E♭7♯11` | Tritone-sub turnaround. The melody pickup, the guitar anticipation, and the brush fill all push into bar 1 of the loop. |

## Technique checklist

| Technique | Bars |
|---|---|
| **Line cliché** | 1–4, 9–12, 25–28: the guitar's inner voice moves D–C♯–C–B under `Dm` while bass stays on D. In 9–12 and 25–28 the sax doubles the line in a higher register. |
| **Tritone substitution** | 8 and 32 (E♭7♯11 for A7 → Dm, so bar 32 loops into bar 1), and 20 (A♭7♯11 for D7 → Gm9) |
| **Backdoor ii–V** | 18 (E♭m9–A♭13 → B♭maj9), 22 (B♭m9–E♭13 → Fmaj9) |
| **Rootless A/B voicings** | All 32 bars on guitar, with no roots except the cliché voice. Forms were chosen for the smallest movement, for example `Gm9` B → `C13` A → `Fmaj9` B in bars 5–7, and `E♭m9` A → `A♭13` B in bar 18. |
| **Guide-tone voice leading** | Guitar: 5–7 (F→E→E, B♭→B♭→A), 16 (B♭→A), 18 (D♭→C). Sax: 13–16 and 29–31, plus sustained guide tones in 17–24. |
| **Tension-note landing** | 9th on beat 1 of bars 1, 2, 9, 10, 25, 26. 13th in 4, 6, 12, 14, 18. ♯11 in 8 and 32 (A over E♭7). 9th in 7, 17, 19, 21, 22, 23, and ♭9 in 24. |
| **Guide-tone and compound-line melody** | 5–8, 13–14, 29–32: the upper strand A–B♭–A–B♭–A–G–A alternates with the lower strand F–D–E–E. The bridge melody follows the 3rds and 7ths (A in 17, G♭ in 18). |
| **Gap-fill** | 1 (E→A leap, then G–F), 3 (F→C, then B♭–A), 19 (G♭→C, then A–G–F), 21 (C→A, then G–F–D) |
| **Clave** | All bars: side-stick bossa clave in 3-2, which lines up over the 32-bar loop |
| **Brush and jazz kit idioms** | Brush swirl in 1–8 and on the downbeats of 17–24. Hat foot on 2 and 4 throughout. 8th hats in A2 and A3. Ride plus brush snare comping in 17–24, lighter snare comping in 25–32. Brush fills on the last beat of 8, 16, 24, 32. |
| **Root pulse** | All bars: root on 1 and 5th on 3 (♭5 on m7♭5), both roots in two-chord bars, and a D pedal under the cliché |
| **Comping patterns** | All bars: two-bar batida on nylon guitar, with off-beat hits pushing early into the next chord, including bar 32 into bar 1 |
| **Countermelody** | Tenor sax enters at A2 (9–16), sustains in the bridge (17–24), and returns in A3 (25–32) |
| **AABA** | A1 1–8, A2 9–16 (ends with Cm9–F13 into B♭), B 17–24, A3 25–32, then the loop goes back to bar 1 |
| **Panning plan** | Center: flute, bass, and the kick/stick/snare track. Guitar at L 0.4, sax at R 0.35, hat/ride at R 0.25. |
