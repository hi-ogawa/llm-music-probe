This version starts from the full-shape one (gist `6175c1e7f2fd273d48db544b27b67b2e`) and changes one thing. The downstroke on beat 4 of every second bar sits an 8th before the `&4` anticipation into the next bar. Its release is now measured back from the push: the left hand lets go 0.06–0.14 beat (about 30–65 ms) before the `&4` stroke, chosen per stroke, and each string stops ±0.02 beat around that point, so the cutoff is uneven rather than a single block. The 16th-choke version (gist `1229f084dea198a4078b2dc00fa518f0`) cut after 0.20–0.28 beat, which left about 0.25 beat of rest and felt abrupt.

Everything else rings as in the full-shape version. Pitches, stroke times, velocities and the other tracks are unchanged, and the change touches 16 strokes (79 notes, lengths only). `python3 choke.py fullshapes.json project.json` reproduces it (seeded RNG).

## Iteration

Hiroshi's requests and feedback are quoted or paraphrased from the thread. Each version was A/B'd in Toy MIDI against the ones before it.

1. **Strummed** (gist `40bb09a52ec8b24e3f3c268a5cf1caa2`). The original guitar was keyboard programming with a fixed 17 ms downward roll. Hiroshi asked for strum programming: alternating down/up strokes, varied spread, velocity taper, lighter upstrokes, natural lengths and timing jitter, keeping the pitches. Feedback: "a concrete but minor improvement, one a listener may not notice". **Kept**: the stroke timing, directions, spread and velocity treatment, which every later version builds on.
2. **Re-voiced with a thumb line** (gist `375801d436784457d2f8f9eeb4093381`). Clawd (the main session) proposed and handed off a re-voicing like a bossa guitarist, with the thumb playing root and fifth on the low strings and the fingers plucking 3–4 upper chord tones in playable shapes. A shape search over standard tuning produced one playable shape per chord. **Dropped**: Hiroshi pointed out that it missed his hypothesis. Keyboard-style close voicing is what makes the comping sound like a Rhodes, and moving the low strings into a separate thumb line left the strokes close-voiced, at about 3 notes within a ninth. Only the shapes from the search survived.
3. **Texture on the re-voiced take** (gist `aedaf0432bfa17eb5cb98e951ff9fc22`). Accents, light in-between strokes and chucks on a muted guitar track (GM 28). It was superseded by the voicing correction above before it got its own feedback. **Dropped** along with the re-voicing.
4. **Full shapes** (gist `6175c1e7f2fd273d48db544b27b67b2e`). Hiroshi's test of the hypothesis: each hit plays the full guitar shape as one stroke, with downstrokes on all 5–6 strings and upstrokes on the top 3–4. There's no thumb line, and the strummed stroke treatment is kept. The shapes span 18–29 semitones, against about 14 for the keyboard voicings. **Kept** as the base of the final version.
5. **Full shapes + texture with chucks** (gist `6ff0ec99b53121e8e74f5d5c457441d2`). The texture pass from step 3, applied to the full shapes. Feedback: "a beat of rest intended? it sounds a bit vacant", and you wouldn't wait after a muted strum like that. Cause: every in-between stroke cut the ringing chord and was itself short, so 31 spots of 0.7–1 beat of dead air opened up after `&3` and after beat 3 of the second bar of each 2-bar pattern. **Dropped**: Hiroshi said to drop chucks as too minor to affect listening.
6. **Full shapes + light strokes** (gist `1d239f7cf46f08df55708681d5be5b8c`). No chucks, and the in-between strokes play the top strings lightly while the chord keeps ringing. Feedback: keeping everything ringing is the same as the original, and the real twist is shortening the 8th/16th-note stroke around the bar boundary. **Dropped**: it was too close to the original to test anything.
7. **Full shapes + 16th choke** (gist `1229f084dea198a4078b2dc00fa518f0`). Only the beat-4 stroke an 8th before each `&4` push changes, cut after about a 16th (0.20–0.28 beat). These are 16 strokes, and everything else rings. Feedback: "may feel cut off too abrupt", and it needs a shorter rest with a humanized cutoff. **Superseded**: the idea was kept and the rest shortened.
8. **Full shapes + short release** (this gist). The release is measured back from the push, 0.06–0.14 beat before the `&4`, and each string stops ±0.02 beat around it. Feedback: "we got it". **Final.**
