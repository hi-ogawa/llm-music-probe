# Borrowed Mirrorball (32-bar disco loop)

**[Open in Toy MIDI](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fborrowed-mirrorball%2Fproject.json)**

F major, 120 BPM, 4/4. Loop range covers all 32 bars. The borrowed chords come from F minor (iv B♭m6, ♭VI D♭maj7, ♭VII E♭).

## Plan

| Bars | Section | Chords | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Intro (beat only) | F pedal | Kick on every beat, closed offbeat hats, octave bass on F, crash on bar 1 |
| 3–4 | Intro | F pedal | Claps on 2 and 4, shaker 16ths enter. Lead pickup on bar 4 beat 4-and |
| 5–8 | Verse A | Fmaj7, Am7, B♭maj7, **B♭m6** | Crash, open offbeat hats, string ostinato enters. Low-register lead (C4–B♭4) with off-beat entrances, and D♭ on B♭m6 marks the borrowed iv |
| 9–12 | Verse B | Fmaj7, Dm7, Gm7, **E♭9** | Congas enter, chord stabs on 2-and and 4-and. Kick pulled and snare roll on bar 12 beat 4 |
| 13–16 | Chorus | B♭maj7, Am7, **D♭maj7**, **E♭6** | Lead lifts to C5–A5 and each phrase anticipates the next chord on 4-and. Tambourine and 16th closed hats are added. Stabs anticipate the next chord |
| 17–20 | Chorus | Fmaj7, Dm9, **B♭m6**, C7 | Same hook continued. Kick pulled and snare roll on bar 20 beat 4 |
| 21–23 | Break | **D♭maj7, E♭6, D♭maj7** | Kick, claps, and lead out. 16th hats, percussion tops, strings, and stop-time stabs remain. Bass holds whole-bar roots and then returns to octaves in bar 23 |
| 24 | Break, dropout | **E♭6** | The whole kit, percussion, bass, and strings drop. Only three stabs plus the lead pickup |
| 25–32 | Chorus 2 | Same as 13–20 | Full band plus a flute countermelody filling the downbeats where the lead rests. Bar 32 beat 4 is pulled (kick, clap, hat, bass), so C7 resolves into the F bass and crash at bar 1 |

## Technique checklist

- [x] **Modal interchange**: B♭m6 (iv) in bars 8, 19, 31. D♭maj7 (♭VI) in bars 15, 21, 23, 27. E♭9/E♭6 (♭VII) in bars 12, 16, 22, 24, 28
- [x] **Syncopated and anticipated phrasing**: lead phrases start on 1-and/2-and and land on 4-and ahead of the barline in bars 4–20 and 24–32. Stabs anticipate the next chord on 4-and in bars 13–19 and 25–31
- [x] **Register lift**: verse lead in C4–B♭4 (bars 5–12), and chorus lead in C5–A5 (bars 13–20, 25–32)
- [x] **Four-on-the-floor**: kick on every beat with open offbeat hats and claps on 2 and 4 in bars 1–20 and 25–32 (claps from bar 3)
- [x] **Percussion tops**: shaker from bar 3, congas from bar 9, tambourine in bars 13–20 and 25–32. All are panned right and sit under the kit
- [x] **Octave bass**: alternating root octaves on eighths in bars 1–20, 23, and 25–32
- [x] **String ostinato**: an 8th-note figure on upper chord tones in bars 5–23 and 25–32
- [x] **Chord stabs**: short brass hits in bars 9–24 and 25–32, with stop-time stabs in bars 21–24
- [x] **Countermelody**: a flute line descending D6–C6–C6–B♭5–A5–F5–F5–E5 in bars 25–32, placed in the lead's downbeat rests
- [x] **Density build**: one layer at a time in bars 1 (kick, hats, bass), 3 (claps, shaker), 5 (strings, lead), 9 (congas, stabs), 13 (tambourine, 16th hats), and 25 (countermelody)
- [x] **Form, intro / verse / chorus / break / chorus**: intro 1–4, verse 5–12, chorus 13–20, break 21–24, chorus 25–32. Locators mark each section
- [x] **DJ-friendly phrase blocks**: every section boundary falls on the 4-bar grid, both choruses and the verse are clean 8-bar blocks, and the intro is rhythm section only. The intro and the break are 4-bar half-blocks, so the 32 bars fit the requested five sections
- [x] **Drum dropout**: kick pulled on beat 4 of bars 12, 20, and 32. The whole kit drops in bar 24 before chorus 2
- [x] **Panning plan**: kick, bass, and lead are centered, while strings and flute sit left and stabs and percussion sit right (see the table above)
