## How I read the inspiration

80s city pop (Tatsuro Yamashita, Mariya Takeuchi, Anri) is a groove-first, harmonically lush style. It runs on maj7/9 and m9 chords, a busy octave-popping bass, a cutting guitar on the offbeats, brass stabs that push the & of 4, and a hooky chorus on the "Just the Two of Us" progression (IVmaj7, III7, vim7, then v7 to I7). The last chorus usually goes up a half step.

With no drum kit, each rhythm-section job goes to the orchestra:

- **Bass guitar to pizzicato basses**, which pop octaves in 16ths and slide into each chord with a chromatic approach note. A bowed contrabass under them adds weight in the bigger sections.
- **Cutting guitar to pizzicato chords** on the 16th offbeats.
- **Horn section to brass stabs**, plus a trumpet and trombone soli in the interlude.
- **Keys and pads to low strings, horns, and harp arpeggios.** A glockenspiel stands in for the bell-like DX7 keyboard sparkle.

Form: intro (the chorus hook as a teaser), verse in Eb, pre-chorus lifting through Bb/Ab, chorus, brass interlude that side-slips down to set up E, last chorus in E, and a turnaround (Amaj7, then Bbm7 and Eb7) that lands back on the intro's Abmaj7.

Each section boundary has a build-up: a string scale run, a timpani roll on the dominant, a harp glissando, pizz basses pedaling in 8ths, and brass hits climbing into a push on the & of 4. The big builds are into the chorus, into the key change, and into the loop.
