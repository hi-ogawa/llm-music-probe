# Guitar idiom tests in MIDI: bends

**Demo: [open v3 with bends in Toy MIDI](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv3-with-bends.json)** ([v3 picked](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv3-picked.json), [v2 with bends](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv2-with-bends.json), [v2 picked](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv2-picked.json), [v1 with bends](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv1-with-bends.json), [v1 picked](https://toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Fmain%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Fv1-picked.json)) (plays GeneralUser without the bends, not the Telecaster renders)

One articulation per test: a short phrase written so that the articulation belongs there, rendered with and without it, so the difference can only come from that articulation. This one tests bends. Context: https://github.com/hi-ogawa/toy-midi/issues/861 (verification table), after the hammer-on test on the Interlock lead (https://github.com/hi-ogawa/llm-music-probe/tree/main/compositions/pieces/interlock-in-e). Bends were deferred there because hammer-ons are the easiest articulation to judge first, not because bends don't matter.

Each version is a pair of project files with Toy MIDI links. Toy MIDI has no articulation support yet, so it plays the notes without the bends, on GeneralUser. The renders are MP3s in the Discord thread, not kept here.

## Amp Version

**[Open the amp version in Toy MIDI](https://fix-issue-974-toy-midi.hiro18181.workers.dev/draft?src=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2Fhi-ogawa%2Fllm-music-probe%2Famp-rearrangements%2Fcompositions%2Fpieces%2Fbend-test%2Famp.json)**, on the preview of https://github.com/hi-ogawa/toy-midi/pull/977, the PR that adds the amp, so the link stops working once that preview is gone.

`amp.json` is `v3-with-bends.json` with the high gain amp on the lead. Drive holds a note up while it bends, which is where bends sound most guitar-like, so this is also the first version whose bends play in Toy MIDI: the `art` marks are written as pitch-bend automation, picked at the lower pitch and bent up, with each release continuing the bent note back down. The amped track moves to the Telecaster bridge SoundFont the amp was tuned on, gets CC 11 cuts after each note-off so the drive doesn't bring a note's release up into the next one, and has its gain lowered so it plays at the original's dry loudness. `amp.mjs` builds it.

## Setup

- Font: the [FlameStudios Telecaster neck pickup](https://github.com/gdm-2112/electric-guitar-soundfonts/tree/main/SF3/FlameStudios) SF3 (GPL), the clean default picked in #861. The project files keep GM 27 (and 28 for ghosts), and the render maps them to the font's Standard (and Muted) presets.
- Backing on GeneralUser: electric piano chords, finger bass, and soft drums, Am7 Fmaj7 Dm7 E7 at 76 BPM, played twice.
- Rendering: `render.mjs` from the Night Drive gist (https://github.com/hi-ogawa/llm-music-probe/tree/main/compositions/pieces/night-drive-cutting-guitar). Notes carry `art: "b"` (picked `bend` semitones below and bent up over `bendMs`) and `art: "r"` (released back without a new pick), plus `vib` and `vibDelayMs`. On a bent note, the vibrato only dips below the bent pitch, the way a player eases the bend. The renderer gained `bendMs`, `vibDelayMs`, and that vibrato shape for this test. The lead tracks of each pair are level-matched.

## Iteration

1. **Pitches over chords** (`v1-with-bends.json`, `v1-picked.json`, `bend-v1.mjs`). Three whole-step bends with releases (D5 to E5, G4 to A4, A4 to B4), 140 ms up and 110 ms down. Feedback: asked whether it tries the guitar nuance programmed so far. It doesn't: the line is pitches over chords rather than fretboard-first, accents follow a downbeat rule (86 on the beat, 76 off), every note stops exactly at its written length, none of the verified idioms (muted ghosts, phrased hammer-ons) are there, and the held bends have no vibrato. The A/B isolates the bend, but on a phrase that isn't guitar. **Dropped**.
2. **From the fretboard** (`v2-with-bends.json`, `v2-picked.json`, `bend-v2.mjs`). Written as string and fret in the 5th-fret A minor pentatonic box on the G, B, and e strings. Pull-offs and a hammer-on where a player would use them, three muted ghosts in the gaps before new phrases, a monophonic line where each note lasts until the next one (notes before a rest ring their written length), about 8 ms behind the beat, and velocities by phrase. Bends: B string 8 (G4) a whole step to A4 and released, G string 7 (D4) a whole step to E4 (the 9th of Dm7) and released into bar 4, and B string 8 (G4) a half step to G#4 (the major 3rd over E7). Held bends get a vibrato after about 280 ms. In `picked`, the same pitches are fretted and the releases picked, with the same vibrato on held notes, so the bend is the only variable. Feedback: getting better at faking guitar articulation, but with failed ideas. The muted notes have no clear purpose: the ghost idiom works in a funky 16th line where the picking hand keeps moving, and in a slow line three isolated clicks are noise. The vibrato sounds like a tremolo bar wiggle, either musically off or a failed articulation: a perfectly regular 5.5 Hz wave on a fixed delay, on every held note. **Dropped**: the ghosts and the vibrato.
3. **Bends alone** (`v3-with-bends.json`, `v3-picked.json`, `bend.mjs`). v2 without the muted ghosts and without any vibrato, so the pair tests only the bends. Vibrato gets its own test later. Feedback: pending.
