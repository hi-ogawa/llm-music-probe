# Dilla-style instrumental, 88 bpm

A loop-based beat built on call and answer. Everything on top plays a little behind the beat, like a lifted sample.

## Form

- **Intro (1-4):** Rhodes alone. The flute teases the first two notes of the hook in bar 3, and a soft choir pad leads into the drums.
- **Verse A (5-12):** The flute plays the hook (C-D-F-A up, G-E-D-C-A down). The muted trumpet answers at the end of each four-bar phrase. Bar 12 is left empty on top on purpose.
- **Bridge (13-20):** Ride, congas and strings come in. The flute calls and the trumpet answers one bar later (13/14, 18/19). Bars 15-16 belong to the flute, and bar 17 is a deliberate rest. Bar 20's E-C-C# over A7#9 hands off to the breakdown.
- **Breakdown (21-24):** Sub bass, brushes and Rhodes carry it alone. The flute's C-D tease from bar 3 comes back in bar 22.
- **Verse A2 (25-32):** The 808, claps, shaker and organ come in. Vibes carry the hook for two bars in a new colour, and then the flute returns with the C6 peak in bar 29.
- **Outro (33-36):** Everything thins out. The trumpet's last answer hands off to the flute's held D, which rings into the end.

## Mix

- **Center:** kick, snare and all drum kits, both basses, and the choir pad. These carry the pulse and the root, so they stay in the middle.
- **Call left, answer right:** the flute, and the vibes when they carry the hook, sit at -0.4. The muted trumpet sits at +0.45. The hook always comes from the same place even when its colour changes, and every answer arrives from the other side.
- **Harmony:** Rhodes is just left of center (-0.15). The sustained beds sit right (+0.3), with strings in the bridge and organ in verse A2, so the comping and the pad don't stack in one spot.
- **Hand percussion** (congas, shaker, tambourine) at +0.3, beside the kit rather than inside it.
