## How I read the inspiration

What I took from Daft Punk is the French-house method more than any one song. A single four-bar loop of lush jazz-tinged chords (Fm9, Dbmaj9, Bbm9, C7) runs under the whole track, and the drama comes from what gets added to or taken away from the loop, not from new harmony. The hook is a short, robotic, syncopated brass riff that repeats until it becomes the point, in the spirit of "One More Time" and "Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger", with some *Tron: Legacy* string ostinato mixed in.

Without a drum kit, each part of the kit went to an orchestral role:

- **Kick**: timpani on all four beats, tuned to the chord root
- **Clap**: orchestra hit on 2 and 4 in the choruses
- **Hi-hats**: violin 16th-note spiccato, accented on the off-beat 8ths like an open hat
- **Disco bass**: pizzicato octave jumps
- **Guitar chops**: staccato string chords in a syncopated Nile Rodgers-ish pattern

I can't automate a filter sweep in a MIDI-only orchestra, so the intro fakes one. Voices enter low and quiet, then velocities and register rise. Each build-up speeds the timpani up (quarters, then 8ths, 16ths, and triplets), climbs a violin tremolo through the F minor scale, swells the horns on the dominant, and then cuts everything for half a beat before the drop. That hole is the most Daft Punk move in the piece.
